THE WELLBORE FLUID RACE
Oil, gas and pressure inside production tubing

OPEN IN GODOT

1. Extract the complete ZIP to a folder.
2. In Godot 4.7, choose Import in the Project Manager and select project.godot.
3. Open the project and press F5. Godot imports the assets on first open.
4. Choose Manual race to operate the well, or Guided race to watch the
   approximately 100-second exhibition demonstration.

CONTROLS

In Manual race, press Drop pressure once to cross the bubble point and see
smaller gas bubbles. Drop pressure again at the default choke opening to
see elongated gas pockets in slug flow. Adjust the choke opening to explore
how flow resistance changes the display. Restore pressure to bring the
example back above the bubble point. The language button switches the HUD
and subtitles between English and Arabic. The character voices are English.

Space     Start a manual race from idle
D         Drop pressure during a manual race
P         Pause or resume
R         Reset the race
Left/Right or right-drag    Turn the camera
C         Reset the camera view
F11       Toggle fullscreen

SCIENCE AND AUDIO

The simulation is a qualitative teaching example. Its pressure and
superficial velocity readings use illustrative values. Free-gas volume is
estimated using an idealized inverse-pressure relationship. Choke response
and flow-regime thresholds are specific to this example. The characters'
race illustrates gas and liquid motion. Their speeds are stylized.

The project includes English recordings for Olly, Gizmo and the narrator,
Arabic and English subtitles, original procedural sound effects, and an
original music loop. It runs offline once downloaded. The Python scripts
under tools/ regenerate audio assets. Voice regeneration needs edge-tts
and FFmpeg. Music regeneration needs FFmpeg. Running the game needs Godot.
Font notices are in assets/fonts/NOTICE.txt.
